What the two large partials mean

e.os at 37 of 121 and e.io at 57 of 57 are not stalled work. They are exactly the subsets the compiler needs in order to build itself. The rest of e.os waits on extern with @cc. e.io no longer waits on printf: that expands, over a 4 KiB buffer of its own drained through a generated sink.

e.str is complete at 66 of 66 and is the first module at surface: "source" to have needed the compiler for any of it. format expands, and so does push_err — the one declaration a library could not write, because what it prints is the qualified name and the merged error table is a property of the whole program, where a module sees one module at a time. What the expansion still cannot reach is a slice, an array or a named type's own format, each of which needs it to recurse into an argument.

What e.io's remaining declarations wait on is not a compiler feature but a decision. Ten of its constructors have to supply a callback of their own, and the shape they need — fn(*void, []u8) -> (usize, err) — is not one the frozen surface declares. The language has no visibility mechanism (spec §12), so a helper cannot be added quietly: it would be a public symbol the plan does not know about. printf's own sink hit exactly this and was answered by generating the function in the compiler, which is one of the three ways the rest could go; widening the surface and making the constructors intrinsics are the others.

Method

Each capability scores 1 when delivered, 0 when not started, and a fraction between when partial, with the reason stated in its own row. Items are weighted equally inside a dimension; no dimension is weighted against another, and the three numbers are never combined into one. Compiler scope is the CPU language of M1 and M2 — GPU (M3) and Metal (M5) are separate milestones, excluded rather than counted as zero.

Module readiness counts declarations present in committed source or seeded as compiler intrinsics. Uncommitted work in the tree is deliberately not counted.

One judgement is worth naming. Self-hosting is three rows out of 53 here, but it is the binary M2 exit gate. Read the compiler number as capability coverage, not as distance to the milestone.

Keeping this current

This page is generated, never hand-edited. Every session that lands a capability updates it: move the affected rows in the tables at the top of scripts/render_progress.py, run python scripts/render_progress.py from the repository root, and commit the regenerated page with the change that earned it. The module numbers need no editing at all — they are read from docs/module-apis.md, docs/modules.json, the committed lib/e sources and the intrinsics seeded in src/resolve.e.
